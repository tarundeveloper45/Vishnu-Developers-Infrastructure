About Vishnu City

Comfort, Security and Lasting Value

Vishnu City is a residential development by Vishnu Developers & Infrastructure, created around the idea of modern luxury, comfortable living, thoughtful planning, and a secure community environment.

The project offers thoughtfully designed 3 BHK residences, with the featured floor plan covering approximately 1,395.40 sq. ft. The project's area and development plan is organised around a phased layout — internal roads, landscaped gardens and a shared clubhouse — designed to give every residence practical connectivity and a settled, secure environment to grow into.

Infrastructure

Smart & Comfortable Infrastructure

Vishnu City combines residential comfort with practical infrastructure. The development incorporates gated access, CCTV surveillance, security personnel, wide internal roads, solar-powered street lighting, separate sewer systems, lift access, and stilt parking.

The project also provides a thoughtfully designed vertical circulation system, with the brochure specifying a modern staircase and lift provision.

Designed for modern families

The Living Experience at Vishnu City

Every element of Vishnu City is intended to support a lifestyle that is comfortable, secure, spacious, and connected to modern conveniences. From the planning of the 3 BHK residences to the community infrastructure, the project focuses on creating a refined residential environment where families can experience contemporary living with a sense of comfort and pride.

Secure & Settled

Gated access, round-the-clock surveillance and on-site security create an environment families can rely on every day.

Connected Community

Shared roads, gardens and a clubhouse give neighbours natural places to meet, supporting a genuine sense of community.

Everyday Convenience

Lift access, stilt parking, power backup and wide internal roads are planned in from the start, not added later.
