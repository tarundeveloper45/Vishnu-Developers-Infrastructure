About Vishnu City

Vishnu City is a residential development by Vishnu Developers & Infrastructure, created around the idea of modern luxury, comfortable living, thoughtful planning, and a secure community environment. The project offers thoughtfully designed 3 BHK residences, with the featured floor plan covering approximately 1,395.40 sq. ft., organised around a phased layout — internal roads, landscaped gardens and a shared clubhouse — planned through to the project's final phase.

The development is planned around gated access, 24×7 CCTV surveillance, day and night security, 9-metre-wide CC roads, LED solar street lighting, a separate sewer system, Vastu-oriented planning, lift facility and stilt parking — supported by finance and loan facilities through leading nationalised banks and financial institutions.

Vishnu City is designed to support a lifestyle that is comfortable, secure, and connected to modern conveniences, while contributing positively to the surrounding community through thoughtful infrastructure, shared spaces and a settled, secure environment for residents to grow into.
