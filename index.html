03 Our legacy

A heritage of enterprise & service

Progress is
in our foundations.

Our story carries the pioneering spirit of generations who built institutions, advanced knowledge and served their communities.

Freedom-era roots

Late Pandit Vishnu Dutt Sharma, a renowned Jyotish Acharya from BHU Banaras and freedom fighter, established the family tradition of public service.

Healthcare innovation

Late Dr. Onkar Dutt Sharma founded Martand Pharmacy and contributed to Ayurvedic research, publishing and pharmaceutical enterprise.

Diversified enterprise

The legacy expanded across cinema, education, fuel retail, hospitality and Baraut's first multiplex — Vishnu Palace iMusica.

A new chapter

Today, Vishnu Developers & Infrastructure carries this spirit forward into responsible, future-ready development.
