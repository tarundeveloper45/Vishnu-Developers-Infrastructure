Abaxis Design and Build Private Limited

Abaxis Design & Build Pvt. Ltd., established in 2004, is a multidisciplinary design and construction firm offering integrated expertise in Architecture, Interior Design, Civil Construction, MEP, and Project Management.

We bring together creative thinking, technical expertise, and execution capabilities to deliver functional, sustainable, and enduring spaces. Our integrated design-to-delivery approach ensures seamless coordination from concept to completion, while maintaining a strong focus on quality, cost efficiency, and timely execution.

With experience across institutional, public-sector, corporate, and private projects, Abaxis is committed to creating spaces that balance design, functionality, technology, and long-term value.

From vision to reality, we design, build, and deliver with purpose.
