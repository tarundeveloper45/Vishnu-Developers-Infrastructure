Abaxis Design and Build Pvt. Ltd.

Established in 2004 and headquartered in Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi, Abaxis Design and Build Pvt. Ltd. is a team of architects, engineers, project managers and contractors offering turnkey services in architecture, civil, interiors and project management. The firm is ISO certified and BIFMA Level 3 certified for furniture, with a portfolio spanning residential, commercial, institutional, industrial and healthcare projects for clients including public-sector and corporate organisations.
